Virat Kohli (born November 5, 1988) is a legendary Indian cricketer known as one of the greatest batsmen in history. Rising from Delhi, he led India to the 2008 U-19 World Cup victory. Making his international debut in 2008, he became a core team member and was named captain in 2014. Renowned for his fitness, technical skill, and incredible chase ability, he has scored the most ODI centuries and has over 85 international tons. Known as "King Kohli," his leadership saw India achieve significant success, including historic Test series wins abroad.
